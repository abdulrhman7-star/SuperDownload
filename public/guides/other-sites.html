Other Supported Websites: Guide

Follow these steps to work with a public other-site website link. Instructions are educational; actual availability depends on the source.

Use content responsibly. Only save videos you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. This guide does not grant rights to someone else's content.

How to copy a Other Sites video link

  1. Open the specific public page containing the video.
  2. Copy the page URL from the Share menu or browser address bar.
  3. Paste the link into SuperDownload and choose Find video.
  4. If the page is not recognized, check the site’s official download options.

How to use SuperDownload

  1. Open the SuperDownload home page in your browser.
  2. Paste the public link into the link field and select Find video.
  3. If the source is supported, review the title and other available details.
  4. Choose an available output option and start the download.

Helpful tips

  • Use the canonical page address instead of a search result or shortened redirect when possible.
  • Try a different browser only if the link itself appears incomplete.
  • Report repeatable failures through the contact page, without sending passwords or private links.

Known limitations

SuperDownload relies on third-party extraction software, so support is not universal and may change without notice. We do not bypass DRM, paywalls, logins, or technical access controls. Compatibility can change when a platform updates its website, requires sign-in, restricts a region, or changes its delivery methods. SuperDownload cannot guarantee that every link will work.
