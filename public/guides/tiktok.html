TikTok Video Download Guide

Follow these steps to work with a public tiktok link. Instructions are educational; actual availability depends on the source.

Use content responsibly. Only save videos you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. This guide does not grant rights to someone else's content.

How to copy a Tiktok video link

  1. Open the video in TikTok.
  2. Tap Share and choose Copy link.
  3. Paste the link into the SuperDownload page and select Find video.

How to use SuperDownload

  1. Open the SuperDownload home page in your browser.
  2. Paste the public link into the link field and select Find video.
  3. If the source is supported, review the title and other available details.
  4. Choose an available output option and start the download.

Helpful tips

  • Make sure the link is complete and refers to a specific video.
  • If the video owner removes it or changes availability, try no more than once after confirming the link.
  • Respect the creator’s rights and any applicable platform rules.

Known limitations

Private videos, restricted accounts, age or region restrictions, and some delivery formats may not be supported. Watermarks and available formats depend on the source; no watermark-removal feature is promised. Compatibility can change when a platform updates its website, requires sign-in, restricts a region, or changes its delivery methods. SuperDownload cannot guarantee that every link will work.
