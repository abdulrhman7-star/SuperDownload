Facebook Video Download Guide

Follow these steps to work with a public facebook link. Instructions are educational; actual availability depends on the source.

Use content responsibly. Only save videos you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. This guide does not grant rights to someone else's content.

How to copy a Facebook video link

  1. Open the video post on Facebook.
  2. Open the post menu and select Copy link, or copy the post URL from your browser address bar.
  3. Paste the full link into SuperDownload and check whether the source is available.

How to use SuperDownload

  1. Open the SuperDownload home page in your browser.
  2. Paste the public link into the link field and select Find video.
  3. If the source is supported, review the title and other available details.
  4. Choose an available output option and start the download.

Helpful tips

  • Copy the link to the video post itself rather than a general profile or group page.
  • A video shared only with friends or a closed group may not be accessible.
  • Do not use saved copies to misrepresent the creator or remove attribution.

Known limitations

Private-group videos, login-required content, live streams, removed posts, and some regional or age restrictions may prevent processing. Compatibility can change when a platform updates its website, requires sign-in, restricts a region, or changes its delivery methods. SuperDownload cannot guarantee that every link will work.
