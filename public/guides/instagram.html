Instagram Video Download Guide

Follow these steps to work with a public instagram link. Instructions are educational; actual availability depends on the source.

Use content responsibly. Only save videos you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. This guide does not grant rights to someone else's content.

How to copy a Instagram video link

  1. Open the Instagram app or website and navigate to the post or Reel.
  2. Use the post menu and choose Copy link, if that option is available.
  3. Check that the copied address points to the intended public post.

How to use SuperDownload

  1. Open the SuperDownload home page in your browser.
  2. Paste the public link into the link field and select Find video.
  3. If the source is supported, review the title and other available details.
  4. Choose an available output option and start the download.

Helpful tips

  • A link to a profile is different from a link to an individual post or Reel.
  • If a creator has changed visibility or removed a post, the link may stop working.
  • Respect creator permissions and do not redistribute a saved video without permission.

Known limitations

Private accounts, Stories, login-required posts, music restrictions, and some Reels may not be accessible. The tool does not access private accounts or bypass access controls. Compatibility can change when a platform updates its website, requires sign-in, restricts a region, or changes its delivery methods. SuperDownload cannot guarantee that every link will work.
