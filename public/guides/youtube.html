YouTube Video Download Guide

Follow these steps to work with a public youtube link. Instructions are educational; actual availability depends on the source.

Use content responsibly. Only save videos you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise legally allowed to save. This guide does not grant rights to someone else's content.

How to copy a Youtube video link

  1. Open the YouTube video page.
  2. Select Share and copy the video link, or copy its URL from your browser.
  3. Paste the link into SuperDownload to check whether the video can be processed.

How to use SuperDownload

  1. Open the SuperDownload home page in your browser.
  2. Paste the public link into the link field and select Find video.
  3. If the source is supported, review the title and other available details.
  4. Choose an available output option and start the download.

Helpful tips

  • The selected resolution may affect file size and processing time.
  • A source may offer separate video and audio streams that need to be merged.
  • Use YouTube’s official offline features when appropriate and follow applicable terms.

Known limitations

Some videos, live streams, age-restricted material, DRM-protected media, and sign-in-required content may not work. Downloading may be restricted by YouTube’s terms or copyright law; only proceed when authorized. Compatibility can change when a platform updates its website, requires sign-in, restricts a region, or changes its delivery methods. SuperDownload cannot guarantee that every link will work.
