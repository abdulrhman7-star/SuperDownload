Contact and Report an Issue

We welcome reports about broken pages, accessibility problems, incorrect information, and potential misuse.

What to include

  • A short description of the issue and the page where you encountered it.
  • The device and browser you used, if relevant.
  • Any error message shown by the site.

Protect your privacy

Do not send passwords, session cookies, private video links, government IDs, payment information, or other sensitive data. If reporting a source link, share only a public URL you are allowed to disclose.

Site owner contact

The site owner has not configured a public support email yet. Before launch, set SUPPORT_EMAIL in public/contact.js to an inbox that is checked regularly.

A working contact method should be configured before applying for advertising or accepting user reports.

Response times

This is a small utility project and no specific response time is guaranteed. If you operate the site, check the configured inbox regularly and respond to legitimate copyright, privacy, and security reports.
