Audio Extraction and MP3

Understand the audio-only option and when converting a video to MP3 may be available.

What audio-only means

For supported sources, SuperDownload can request an audio-only output and use FFmpeg to convert the available audio stream to MP3. This depends on the source exposing an accessible audio stream and on the server having enough resources to process it.

How to use the option

  1. Paste a public video URL on the home page and select Find video.
  2. When the video is available, choose Audio only (MP3) from the output selector.
  3. Start the download and wait for processing to finish.

Quality and file size

MP3 is a lossy audio format. The output size and quality depend on the source audio and conversion settings; converting a low-quality source cannot restore missing detail.

Limitations

Audio extraction may fail for protected, private, unsupported, or very large media. The free demo server also has processing and file-size limits. This service does not remove DRM or other access controls.

Use audio files only when you have the necessary rights. Music and other recordings may be protected even when they can be played publicly.
