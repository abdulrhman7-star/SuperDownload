Compatibility guide

Supported Websites and Limitations

Learn how source compatibility works and why a video link may not always be downloadable.

What “supported” means

SuperDownload uses third-party extraction software to identify media on some public pages. Support varies by website, content type, region, account requirements, and changes made by the source platform.

Platforms with guides

InstagramFacebookTikTokThreadsXYouTubeOther websites

Common reasons a link fails

  • The post is private, removed, or restricted.
  • The website requires an authenticated session.
  • The platform changed its page or streaming format.
  • The host is temporarily unavailable or rate-limiting requests.
  • The video exceeds the current demo server’s limits.

What to do

Check that the link points to a public post, try the platform’s official features, and avoid repeated requests if the source is blocking access. We do not bypass logins, paywalls, DRM, or technical restrictions.
