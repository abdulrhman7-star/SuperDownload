Video Quality and File Formats

Choose a practical output quality and understand MP4, MP3, resolution, and file size.

Resolution in plain language

Resolution describes the pixel dimensions of a video. Higher resolutions can look sharper on large screens, but usually need more storage and bandwidth. A 720p or 480p output can be more practical for phones and limited connections.

Available options

  • Best available (MP4): requests a high-quality compatible video and audio combination where possible.
  • Up to 720p, 480p, or 360p: limits the requested video height to a maximum level; the source may offer less.
  • MP4: requests an MP4-compatible option when available.
  • Audio only (MP3): requests audio extraction and conversion for supported sources.

Why quality options can differ

The source site controls which streams are available. Some services store video and audio separately, some do not expose every resolution, and some restrict automated access. A format label is not a promise of a particular bitrate.

Choosing the right option

Choose a lower resolution if you need a smaller file or faster transfer. Choose the best available option when quality matters and you have sufficient storage. If playback fails, use a format supported by your device’s media player.
