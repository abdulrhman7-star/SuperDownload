Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current demo implementation. Review and update it to reflect your final hosting, analytics, advertising, and contact configuration before launch.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Information you submit

When you use the downloader, the video page URL you submit is sent to our server so it can request media information or process a download. Do not submit private links, credentials, session cookies, or URLs that reveal sensitive information.

Temporary processing

For download requests, the current application creates a temporary directory on the server, processes the requested media, and attempts to delete the temporary files when the response ends or an error occurs. Temporary processing is not permanent cloud storage. Hosting providers may maintain infrastructure, security, or access logs under their own policies.

Technical data and security

The web server and hosting provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, request time, user agent, and error logs for security and service operation. The app applies basic request limits and URL validation, but no online service can promise absolute security.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current source code does not intentionally set advertising cookies or include an analytics integration. If analytics, Google AdSense, or another advertising provider is added later, this policy must be updated before activation to explain the providers used, cookies or similar technologies, personalization choices, and applicable consent requirements. Third-party providers may process data according to their own policies.

Third-party websites

When you submit a source URL, the application contacts that website or its media endpoints through the extraction tool. The source platform may process requests according to its own privacy policy and terms. SuperDownload does not control those services.

Children

This tool is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal or private information through the service.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit a URL and can stop using the site at any time. For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page. Before public launch, the site owner should add a real monitored contact address and confirm the retention practices of the selected host.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its integrations change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
