Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about using the service and understanding its limitations.

Is SuperDownload free?

The current project is offered as a simple web utility. Hosting capacity and usage limits depend on the deployment plan; free hosting may sleep or impose resource limits.

Which websites work?

Some public links from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, X, YouTube, and other sites may work. Support changes and is never guaranteed. See our compatibility guide.

Why does my link fail?

The post may be private, removed, region-restricted, login-required, or unsupported. Check the URL and the platform’s official options; do not repeatedly retry a blocked link.

Can I download private videos?

No. The service is designed for publicly accessible media and does not bypass privacy settings, logins, DRM, or access controls.

Can I choose the resolution?

When a source exposes compatible formats, the tool may offer best available, MP4, up to 720p, 480p, or 360p, and an audio-only MP3 option. Actual output depends on the source.

Why is the file large or slow?

Higher resolution usually increases file size. Processing time also depends on the source, network, and hosting resources. The demo server has file-size and time limits.

Do you store downloaded videos?

The current server implementation processes downloads in temporary server storage and attempts to remove the temporary files after the response finishes. This is not a guarantee against all failures or host-level logs; read the Privacy Policy.

Can I republish downloaded content?

Not automatically. Publicly viewable does not mean copyright-free. You are responsible for permissions, attribution, applicable law, and platform terms.

Is SuperDownload affiliated with the platforms listed?

No. SuperDownload is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with the platforms mentioned on this site.

How do I report a problem?

Visit the Contact page. Include the public page URL only if you have permission to share it, plus a short description of the issue. Never send passwords or private account information.
