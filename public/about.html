About us

About SuperDownload

SuperDownload is an independent utility project designed to make public-media link processing easier to understand.

Our purpose

SuperDownload aims to provide a straightforward interface for checking public video links and choosing available output options. We also publish practical guides so visitors can understand the process, its limitations, and responsible ways to use media tools.

How the tool works

When a visitor submits a URL, the server validates the address and uses third-party extraction software to request media information. If the source is supported, the interface displays the details returned by that source. A download request may then process the media temporarily on the server.

What we do not promise

  • We do not guarantee that every site, post, format, or resolution will work.
  • We do not claim affiliation with Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, X, YouTube, or other source platforms.
  • We do not intend to bypass DRM, logins, paywalls, private-account restrictions, or other access controls.

Our content principles

Our help pages focus on practical instructions, clear limitations, and respect for creators’ rights. We encourage users to prefer official offline features when appropriate and to obtain permission before reusing someone else’s work.

Get in touch

For feedback, broken-link reports, or questions about these pages, visit Contact. Do not send passwords, session cookies, or private media URLs.
